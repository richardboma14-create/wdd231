NP Membership Benefits

No membership fee for nonprofit organizations.

  • Access to chamber networking events.
  • Community engagement opportunities.
  • Access to selected training resources.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Affordable support for growing businesses.

  • Access to networking events.
  • Business development resources.
  • Member directory listing.

Silver Membership Benefits

Greater visibility and business opportunities.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Discounts on selected chamber events.
  • Additional business promotion opportunities.
  • Access to selected training sessions.

Gold Membership Benefits

Premium benefits for established businesses.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Priority business spotlight opportunities.
  • Premium advertising opportunities.
  • Invitations to exclusive networking events.
